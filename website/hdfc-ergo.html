About HDFC Ergo

HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company is a joint venture between HDFC Ltd. (India's premier housing finance company) and ERGO International AG, the primary insurance entity of Munich Re Group. Established in 2002, it has grown into India's 3rd largest private general insurer.

HDFC Ergo is best known for its Optima health plans, which offer cashless treatment at 13,000+ hospitals, and its comprehensive motor insurance with one of the industry's widest garage networks. The company processes most claims digitally within 30 minutes.
