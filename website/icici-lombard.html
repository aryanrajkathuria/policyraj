About ICICI Lombard

ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company was established in 2001 as a joint venture between ICICI Bank and Canada-based Fairfax Financial Holdings. It is the largest private general insurer in India by gross written premium.

Known for its technology-driven approach, ICICI Lombard was among the first Indian insurers to launch an AI-powered claims process. Their iHealth Plus plan offers coverage up to ₹2 Crore — one of the highest in health insurance.
