What is an Endowment Policy?

An endowment policy is a life insurance plan that combines protection with savings. You pay premiums for a fixed term (10, 15, 20 years). At the end, you receive a maturity benefit (sum assured + bonuses). If you die during the term, your family receives the death benefit.

Simply put: Whether you live or die, your money is safe. If you live to maturity — you get the money. If you don't — your family gets the money.

Endowment policies are one of the most popular traditional insurance products in India, especially from LIC. They offer guaranteed, risk-free returns with life coverage built in.

How Endowment Works — Simple Example

You buy a ₹10L endowment policy for 20 years, paying ₹40,000/year premium
If you survive 20 years: receive ₹10L sum assured + accumulated bonuses (₹15–20L total)
If you pass away in year 5: family receives ₹10L immediately
Your ₹40K/year premium qualifies for 80C deduction — saving ₹8,000–12,000/year in tax
Maturity amount is completely tax-free under Section 10(10D)

Types of Endowment Plans

🏆

Traditional Endowment (LIC / Private)

The classic "savings + protection" plan. Guaranteed sum assured + annual reversionary bonus + terminal bonus at maturity. Zero market risk. LIC's Jeevan Anand, Jeevan Labh are popular examples.

  • 100% guaranteed returns
  • Annual bonus + terminal bonus
  • Loan facility against policy
  • Suitable for all risk profiles
⚡

Limited Premium Endowment

Pay premiums for a shorter period (5 or 10 years) but coverage continues for the full policy term (20–25 years). Higher annual premium but you finish paying early. Good if you expect income to drop later.

  • Pay for 5–10 years, covered for 20+
  • No premium burden in retirement
  • Full guaranteed maturity benefit
🏦

Unit Linked Endowment (ULIP)

Market-linked endowment where your premium is invested in equity/debt funds. Returns are market-linked and subject to fund performance — not guaranteed. Best for those with 15+ year horizon and moderate risk appetite. Past performance is not indicative of future returns.

  • Higher potential returns
  • Switch between equity and debt
  • Tax-free switches inside the plan

Pros & Cons: Honest Assessment

We believe in transparency — here's an honest look at endowment policies.

Why Endowment Makes Sense

  • Zero market risk — guaranteed returns no matter what
  • Forced savings discipline — you MUST save every year
  • Life cover included — no need for separate term plan
  • Tax-free maturity under 10(10D)
  • Loan facility — borrow against policy without breaking it

Limitations to Know

  • Lower returns (5–6%) vs equity investments (12–15%)
  • Long lock-in (10–20 years) — not suitable for short-term needs
  • Surrendering early results in penalties and low returns
  • Life cover amount is often insufficient for today's needs
  • Not ideal as sole investment — best used with term plan + ELSS

Endowment policies get a bad reputation online because people compare them to pure equity returns — that's not a fair comparison. An endowment is for someone who wants GUARANTEED, RISK-FREE savings with life cover built in. If you're disciplined about investing and can handle market risk, buy a term plan + ELSS — you'll get higher returns. But if you're someone who needs forced savings discipline and hates market volatility — an endowment is perfect for you. It won't make you rich, but it will ensure you have a guaranteed corpus at the end of 20 years with your family protected throughout. I recommend LIC Jeevan Anand specifically — it's the only endowment plan that continues to cover you for LIFE even after the premium-paying term ends.
