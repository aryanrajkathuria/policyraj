Insurance Guidance & Updates

Explore each insurance type, know what it covers, and stay updated with the latest market changes.

General Insurance Property, travel, motor, business & more.

General insurance protects against loss, damage, or liability for assets and everyday risks. It includes motor, home, travel, marine, and commercial covers.

  • Motor and home policies cover accident damage, theft, fire, and natural disasters.
  • Travel insurance protects medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and baggage loss.
  • Business cover shields stock, machinery, and liability risks.

Update: IRDAI is promoting faster claim settlement through digital document upload and cashless repair approvals.

Life Insurance Safety net for family & long-term protection.

Life insurance offers financial security for your family when you are not around. It includes term plans, endowment plans, whole life, and ULIPs.

  • Term insurance provides high cover at an affordable premium.
  • Endowment plans combine protection with savings.
  • ULIPs offer market-linked growth along with insurance protection.

Update: New IRDAI guidelines encourage clearer disclosure of charges and better online plan comparison tools.

Term Insurance Pure protection with high sum assured.

Term insurance is a pure life cover that pays a death benefit if the policyholder passes away during the policy term. It is ideal for income protection and liability coverage.

  • Choose a term that covers your key financial goals such as home loan, education, and family expenses.
  • Riders like critical illness, accidental death, and waiver of premium can be added.
  • Premiums are generally lower compared to other life plans.

Update: Many insurers now offer digital underwriting and faster online approvals for healthy applicants.

Investment Grow savings while securing life cover.

Investment-linked insurance products help you build wealth as you stay protected. These include ULIPs, endowment plans, and pension products.

  • ULIPs combine market investment with life cover, letting you choose equity or debt exposure.
  • Endowment plans guarantee maturity benefits and can help save for long-term goals.
  • Regular review of your investment mix helps keep your returns aligned with goals.

Update: Insurers are improving transparency for charges in investment products and allowing easier online switching of fund options.

Health Insurance Medical cover for individuals and families.

Health insurance covers hospitalisation, surgeries, diagnostics, and day-care procedures. It can also include maternity, critical illness, and wellness benefits.

  • Choose family floater or individual plans based on your family structure.
  • Check for cashless hospital networks and pre-existing condition waiting periods.
  • Top-up and super top-up plans add extra cover when you need it most.

Update: Insurers are adding more COVID-safe benefits, annual health check-ups, and teleconsultation support.

Other Insurance Specialty covers for niche needs.

Other insurance categories include personal accident, cyber insurance, professional indemnity, and specialised commercial covers.

  • Personal accident insurance provides cash payment for injury, disability, or death due to accidents.
  • Professional indemnity covers legal liability for advice or professional services.
  • Cyber insurance protects businesses against data breach losses and ransomware attacks.

Update: Demand for cyber and professional indemnity covers is growing as digital risks and business liabilities increase.
