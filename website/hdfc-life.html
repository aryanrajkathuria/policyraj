About HDFC Life

HDFC Life Insurance was established in 2000 as a joint venture between HDFC Ltd. and Standard Life Aberdeen (UK). It is one of India's largest and most trusted private life insurers, with 99.3% claim settlement — the highest in the private life insurance sector.

HDFC Life is known for innovation — Click 2 Protect Life was India's first fully online term plan. Their Sanchay Plus offers guaranteed returns of up to 7.5%, making it among the best guaranteed savings products in the market. ProGrowth Plus is a top-rated ULIP for wealth creation.

HDFC Life Claim Process

Intimate Claim

Call 1800-227-227 or notify via HDFC Life app. Assign nominee or legal heir as claimant.

Submit Documents

Death certificate, policy document, identity proof of nominee — all can be submitted digitally.

Claim Review

HDFC Life's claims team reviews documents and may do a medical history check if required.

Payment in 24 Hours

99.3% of claims approved. Payment credited to nominee's bank account within 24 hours.
