Privacy Policy

How PolicyRaj collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the website policyraj.com operated by Sachin Kathuria, IRDAI-licensed insurance advisor. By using this website or submitting any enquiry, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill in the "Get Free Quote" or contact form (name, mobile number, email, insurance type)
  • Use the AI Document Scanner (document images are processed locally — not stored on our servers)
  • Chat with Veera, our AI assistant (conversation history is not stored beyond your session)
  • Call or WhatsApp us directly

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account details, Aadhaar numbers, or medical records through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to:

  • Contact you with insurance quotes, policy information, or to schedule a consultation
  • Respond to your enquiries via phone, email, or WhatsApp
  • Recommend appropriate insurance products based on your stated requirements

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, profiling, or marketing unrelated to insurance services.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared only in the following situations:

  • With IRDAI-regulated insurance companies — only when you explicitly request a quote or policy from a specific insurer
  • Via WhatsApp — when you submit a quote request, the details are sent to Sachin Kathuria's WhatsApp number (919013976999) via the WhatsApp API. WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy applies to that communication
  • As required by law — if required by IRDAI, court order, or applicable Indian law

4. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. This website does not store your personal details in a database — enquiry data is transmitted directly to Sachin Kathuria via WhatsApp or email and is handled in accordance with standard professional confidentiality practices.

The AI Document Scanner processes uploaded images using browser-based technology (Tesseract.js / Google Gemini API). Images are not uploaded to or stored on PolicyRaj's servers. If you use the Gemini AI engine, your document image is sent to Google's API — Google's Privacy Policy applies.

5. Cookies

This website uses only essential browser storage (localStorage) to remember your AI engine preference (Gemini API key). No tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics are used.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information through this site, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable Indian law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information
  • Withdraw consent and opt out of further communications at any time
  • Raise a grievance about how your data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sachin@policyraj.in or call 9013976999. Grievances are acknowledged and addressed within the timelines prescribed by applicable law. If you remain unsatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the DPDP Act.

8. Third-Party Links

This website contains links to insurer websites and third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before submitting any personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
