What is Motor Insurance?

Motor insurance protects your vehicle — car, bike, or commercial vehicle — from financial loss due to accidents, theft, fire, and natural disasters.

In India, Third Party motor insurance is mandatory by law under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. Driving without it is a punishable offence with a fine of ₹2,000 and/or 3 months imprisonment.

A comprehensive plan goes further — it also covers damage to your own vehicle, giving you complete protection in any situation.

Types of Motor Insurance

Understanding these three types will help you make the right choice.

⚠️

Third Party Insurance

Mandatory by law. Covers damage or injury caused to a third party (another person's vehicle, property, or physical injury) due to your vehicle. Does NOT cover your own vehicle's damage.

  • Legally required for all vehicles
  • Very affordable premium
  • Third-party death/injury covered
  • Third-party property damage covered
🛡️

Comprehensive Insurance

Highly recommended. Covers both third-party liability AND your own vehicle against accidents, theft, fire, flood, earthquake, and natural calamities. Complete protection.

  • Own damage + third-party cover
  • Theft, fire, natural calamity covered
  • Best for new or valuable vehicles
  • Add-ons available for extra cover
🔧

Own Damage (OD) Cover

A standalone cover for your own vehicle damage. Useful if you already have third-party cover but want to add own damage protection separately. Available since 2019.

  • Covers your vehicle damage only
  • Must be paired with TP policy
  • Choose add-ons freely

What Does Motor Insurance Cover?

Here's everything a comprehensive motor insurance plan protects you from.

Road Accidents

Damage to your vehicle due to collision, skidding, or any road accident — repair costs fully covered.

Fire & Explosion

Vehicle damage from fire, self-ignition, lightning, or explosion is completely covered under comprehensive plans.

Natural Calamities

Flood, earthquake, hailstorm, landslide, cyclone — all natural disasters that damage your vehicle are covered.

Theft

If your car or bike is stolen, the insured declared value (IDV) of the vehicle is paid to you. Total peace of mind.

Third Party Liability

Damage to another person's vehicle, property, or bodily injury caused by your vehicle — fully covered.

Personal Accident Cover

Compulsory personal accident cover of ₹15 lakh for the owner-driver in case of death or permanent disability.

Popular Add-Ons to Consider

These add-ons make your motor insurance policy truly comprehensive.

Zero Depreciation

Get 100% claim amount without deduction for depreciation. Best for new cars up to 5 years old. Must-have add-on.

Engine Protection

Covers engine damage due to waterlogging, oil leakage, or hydrostatic lock — expenses not covered in standard policy.

Roadside Assistance

24×7 on-road help for towing, battery jump-start, fuel delivery, tyre change, and minor repairs anywhere in India.

NCB Protect

Preserves your No Claim Bonus even after making a claim. NCB gives up to 50% discount on premium — protect it.
