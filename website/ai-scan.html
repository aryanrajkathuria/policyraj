Supported Document Types

Our AI can read and extract details from all major Indian identity and insurance documents.

Aadhaar Card

Extracts name, DOB, address, and 12-digit UID

PAN Card

Extracts name, DOB, father's name, and PAN number

Vehicle RC

Extracts owner name, vehicle number, registration date, fuel type

Insurance Policy

Extracts policy number, insurer, sum assured, dates, insured name

Passport

Extracts full name, DOB, passport number, nationality, expiry

Driving License

Extracts name, DOB, DL number, vehicle class, validity

Tips for Best Scan Results

Follow these simple tips to get accurate extraction every time.

Good Lighting

Place document in bright, even light. Avoid shadows and glare.

Full Document Visible

Ensure all four corners of the document are visible in the frame.

Hold Still / High Res

Avoid blur. Use the highest resolution camera setting available.

Keep It Flat

Lay document flat on a table to prevent distortion and warping.

Dark Background

Place document on a dark surface for better contrast and edge detection.

Always Review

AI is very accurate but always review extracted data before submitting.
