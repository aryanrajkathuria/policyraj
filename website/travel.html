What is Travel Insurance?

Travel insurance protects you against unexpected emergencies that can ruin your trip — and your finances. Whether you're travelling domestically or internationally, things can go wrong: flights get cancelled, bags get lost, accidents happen, and medical emergencies can occur anywhere.

A travel insurance policy reimburses or directly pays for these losses — so your trip is protected from start to finish. It typically costs less than a meal out — yet protects against losses that could cost lakhs of rupees.

Most international travel visas (Schengen, US, UK) actually require proof of travel insurance with minimum $50,000 medical coverage as a visa condition.

What Does Travel Insurance Cover?

A good travel insurance plan protects every aspect of your trip.

Medical Emergency

Hospitalisation, surgery, doctor visits, and medicines abroad — covered up to $50,000–$500,000 depending on plan. Even medical evacuation back to India is included.

Trip Cancellation

If you must cancel your trip due to illness, death in family, or natural disaster, non-refundable costs (flights, hotel bookings) are reimbursed.

Baggage Loss / Delay

If your checked luggage is lost, damaged, or delayed, you receive compensation to buy essential items or replace your belongings.

Flight Delay / Cancellation

If your flight is delayed by more than 6–12 hours, you're compensated for meals, accommodation, and alternative transport arrangements.

Passport Loss

Emergency passport assistance, travel documents replacement, and related expenses if your passport is lost or stolen during travel.

Personal Accident

Lump sum payout in case of accidental death or permanent disability during the trip. Also covers accident-related medical costs.

Types of Travel Insurance Plans

Choose the plan that matches your travel style and destination.

✈️

International Single Trip

Covers one international trip from the day of departure to return. Best for occasional travellers. Choose based on destination (USA/Canada needs higher medical cover).

  • One trip covered end-to-end
  • Choose duration (7, 15, 30 days etc.)
  • Medical + baggage + trip cancellation
🌍

Annual Multi-Trip Plan

Best for frequent travellers. One annual premium covers unlimited international trips up to 30–45 days per trip. Huge savings vs buying per trip.

  • Unlimited trips in 1 year
  • Cost-effective for 3+ trips/year
  • Same comprehensive coverage
🇮🇳

Domestic Travel Insurance

Covers travel within India. Includes flight delay, baggage loss, personal accident, and some medical costs. Affordable and increasingly popular.

  • Covers domestic flights & trains
  • Baggage & delay cover
  • Starts from just ₹100-300 per trip
👴

Senior Citizen Travel

Specially designed for travellers aged 60–85. Higher medical coverage, pre-existing condition coverage for emergencies, and compassionate visit if hospitalised abroad.

  • Available up to age 85
  • Pre-existing disease emergency cover
  • Compassionate visit cover
🎓

Student Travel Insurance

For students going abroad for education. Longer duration (up to 2 years), covers sponsor protection, study interruption, and bail bond along with medical cover.

  • Coverage for 1–2 years
  • Sponsor protection if parent passes
  • Study interruption covered
👨‍👩‍👧

Family Travel Plan

One policy that covers the entire family travelling together — adults and children. Shared coverage with per-person sub-limits. Better value than individual policies.

  • Entire family under one policy
  • Children often covered free
  • Convenient single premium
