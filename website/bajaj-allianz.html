About Bajaj Allianz

Bajaj General Insurance (formerly Bajaj Allianz General Insurance) began in 2001 as a joint venture between Bajaj Finserv Ltd. and Allianz SE. Following Allianz's exit from the venture, it now operates fully under the Bajaj Finserv umbrella — the same products, teams and claim processes, under a new name — and remains one of India's leading general insurers.

Bajaj Allianz consistently achieves one of the highest claim settlement ratios in the industry at 98.4%. They were the first Indian insurer to process motor claims digitally and offer instant policy renewal. Their Health Guard plans are known for broad coverage and zero room-rent sub-limits on premium variants.
