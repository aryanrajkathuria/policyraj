About Niva Bupa

Niva Bupa Health Insurance (formerly Max Bupa) is a joint venture between Niva Capital and Bupa Group (UK), one of the world's largest international health insurance providers. It is India's only major insurer that exclusively focuses on health insurance.

This singular focus means every product, every process, and every claim team is optimised for health — not spread across motor, travel and property. ReAssure 2.0 is their flagship plan which offers unlimited number of claims in a year with full sum insured restoration each time.

Niva Bupa Claim Process

Go to Network Hospital

Visit any of 10,000+ Niva Bupa network hospitals. Show your e-health card at the desk.

Pre-Auth Request

Hospital directly contacts Niva Bupa — no TPA involved. Faster approval timelines.

Cashless Approval

Pre-auth approved usually within 1-2 hours. Emergency situations get instant approval.

Direct Settlement

Niva Bupa settles directly with hospital. You pay only non-admissible charges.
