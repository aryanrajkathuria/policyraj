About LIC of India

Life Insurance Corporation of India was established on September 1, 1956 by an Act of Parliament, by merging 245 insurance companies. It is fully owned by the Government of India and is the largest institutional investor in India's stock market.

With 290 million+ individual policies and ₹43 lakh crore in AUM, LIC is India's financial backbone. Every LIC policy is backed by the sovereign guarantee of the Government of India — making it the safest insurer in the country. LIC settled ₹23,000+ crore in death claims in FY2024.
