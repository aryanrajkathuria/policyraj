What is Health Insurance?

Health insurance is a contract between you and an insurance company. You pay a small amount every year called a premium, and in return, the insurer pays your medical bills when you get sick, injured, or need surgery.

Think of it as a financial safety net. Without health insurance, a single serious illness — like a heart attack, cancer, or accident — can easily cost ₹3–10 lakh or more, wiping out your entire savings overnight.

With health insurance, you can focus on getting better instead of worrying about hospital bills.

What Does Health Insurance Cover?

A good health insurance plan covers far more than just hospital stays.

Hospitalisation

Room rent, ICU charges, surgeon fees, doctor visits, nursing charges — all covered when you are admitted for 24+ hours.

Day Care Procedures

Over 500+ modern procedures like cataract surgery, chemotherapy, dialysis that don't need 24-hour admission are covered.

Pre-Hospitalisation

Medical tests, specialist consultations, and medicines taken 30–60 days before admission are reimbursed.

Post-Hospitalisation

Follow-up consultations, medicines, and recovery costs for 60–90 days after discharge are also covered.

Ambulance Charges

Emergency ambulance costs to transport you to the hospital are covered under most health plans.

Annual Health Check-up

Free yearly preventive health check-up for all insured members — catch problems early before they become expensive.

Types of Health Insurance Plans

Choose the plan that best fits your age, family size, and health needs.

👤

Individual Plan

Covers only one person. Each family member gets their own separate sum insured. Best for single adults or when family members have different health needs.

  • Dedicated sum insured per person
  • Higher coverage per individual
  • No risk of one illness draining the full cover
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Floater Plan

One shared policy for the entire family — spouse, children, and parents. All members share the total sum insured. Best value for young families.

  • One premium covers the whole family
  • Shared pool used by whoever needs it most
  • More affordable than separate policies
👴

Senior Citizen Plan

Specially designed for people aged 60 and above. Higher premium but covers age-related illnesses, pre-existing conditions, and domiciliary treatment.

  • Covers age-related diseases
  • Available up to age 80–85
  • Tax benefit of ₹50,000 for parents
🩺

Critical Illness Plan

Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of serious diseases like cancer, heart attack, kidney failure, or stroke — regardless of actual treatment cost.

  • Lump sum payout on diagnosis
  • Use money for treatment or lost income
  • Covers 34–64 critical conditions
🏥

Super Top-Up Plan

An affordable add-on that activates once your base cover is exhausted. Gives you ₹10–50 lakh additional coverage at very low cost.

  • Very affordable premium
  • Tops up base insurance cover
  • Ideal for high-cost city hospitals
🤰

Maternity Cover

Covers delivery costs, pre & post natal care, and newborn baby coverage. Usually available as a rider with a waiting period of 2–4 years.

  • Normal & C-section delivery covered
  • Newborn baby covered from birth
  • Pre/post-natal expenses included

How to Choose the Right Plan?

Follow these 5 simple steps and you'll never be confused again.

1

Decide Your Sum Insured

Minimum ₹5 lakh per person. Ideally ₹10–25 lakh for families in cities where private hospital costs are high. Remember — medical costs double every 5–7 years, so cover yourself generously.

2

Check Network Hospitals in Your City

Always verify that the insurer has good cashless tie-ups with quality hospitals in your city. The larger the network, the less hassle during emergencies.

3

Understand the Waiting Period

Most policies have a 30-day initial waiting period (no claims), 2–4 year waiting for pre-existing diseases (diabetes, BP etc.), and 2 years for maternity. Buy early — so waiting periods are served while you're healthy.

4

Check Claim Settlement Ratio

A good insurer settles 95%+ of claims. Lower ratios mean claim rejections. Also check if they offer direct cashless settlement or require reimbursement — cashless is far more convenient.

5

Look for Restoration Benefit

This feature refills your sum insured if it gets exhausted during the year. A must-have for families — if one member uses the full cover in January, others still have coverage for the remaining year.
