What is an Annuity Plan?

An annuity is a financial product where you give a lump sum to an insurance company, and in return, they pay you a fixed, guaranteed income every month (or quarter/year) for the rest of your life — or for a fixed period.

Think of it as the opposite of life insurance. Life insurance pays when you die. An annuity pays while you're alive. Together, they provide complete financial security: your family is protected if you die too early, and you are protected if you live too long.

Annuities are the only product in India that guarantees you'll never run out of money, no matter how long you live.

Why Annuities Are the Perfect Retirement Tool

Guaranteed income — no market risk, no investment decisions in old age
You cannot outlive your income — paid until your last breath
Joint-life option covers your spouse after you — they never lose income
"Return of purchase price" option returns corpus to children after both deaths
Immediate annuity starts paying from next month — no waiting

Types of Annuity Plans

Choose based on when you want income and what you want to leave for your family.

⚡

Immediate Annuity

Pay a lump sum now, start receiving monthly income from the very next month. No waiting, no accumulation phase. Best for people at or near retirement who have a corpus ready to deploy.

  • Income starts next month
  • Fixed, guaranteed amount forever
  • Multiple payout frequencies
  • Return of purchase price option
⏰

Deferred Annuity

Accumulate your corpus through regular premiums over 10–20 years, then convert to a pension at a chosen retirement age. Good for those who are still working and want to build AND convert to pension in one plan.

  • Accumulate at your pace
  • Annuity starts at chosen age
  • Tax benefit on premiums (80C)
  • Flexibility in accumulation phase
💑

Joint Life Annuity

Income continues even after the primary annuitant passes away — the surviving spouse continues to receive the same (or reduced) pension for their lifetime. The most complete retirement solution for couples.

  • Spouse protected for life
  • Income never stops for family
  • Multiple joint-life options
  • Return of purchase price to heirs

Annuity vs Other Retirement Options

Annuity Plan

Pros: Guaranteed income, no market risk, cannot outlive income. Cons: Once purchased, locked in — can't withdraw corpus. Income doesn't grow with inflation (unless inflation-linked annuity chosen). Best for: Risk-averse retirees who value certainty.

SWP from Mutual Funds

Pros: Higher potential returns, flexibility to withdraw/adjust. Cons: Market risk — if markets fall badly, corpus erodes. May run out of money. Best for: Younger retirees (55–65) with higher risk tolerance and financial knowledge.

FD / SCSS / PMVVY

Pros: Flexible, can withdraw corpus, FD interest is predictable. Cons: Interest rates fluctuate, taxable interest, requires active renewal. Best for: Short-term parking or as a complement to an annuity.

Expert Advice from Sachin Kathuria

SK

The biggest fear in retirement is outliving your money. An annuity is the only product that completely eliminates this risk. My recommendation: don't put all your retirement corpus in an annuity — put 40–60% into an immediate annuity for guaranteed living expenses, and keep the rest in liquid investments for emergencies and discretionary spending. Always choose the "Return of Purchase Price" option if you want to leave something for your children — yes, the monthly payout is slightly lower, but your entire corpus comes back to your family after your lifetime. And if you're married, always choose joint-life annuity — your spouse deserves income security too.
